Editorial treatment: obvious OCR artifacts, spelling slips, word spacing, and line-end hyphenation were normalized. Mathematical notation has been transcribed as printed, including apparent source mistakes; this is a transcription rather than a mathematically corrected edition. Some notable original issues, retained for source fidelity, follow. This list is not a claim to be a complete mathematical errata list.

138: In the Poincare inequivalence proof, the source uses u as the variable of the one-variable Schwarz-lemma functions; part 2 uses f_j and Df although the paragraph starts with g. These inconsistencies are retained.
140: The type-IV domain is printed as |sum z_i^2| > sqrt(2 sum |z_i|^2 - 1), without the other restriction normally needed for the bounded component. Retained.
141: The map from the Siegel half-space to the ball has z_j sqrt(2)/(t+i) in the original. Retained.
143–144: The construction of the proper embedding includes the printed factor ((n-1)/n)b_n z and sum beginning j=1 although the preceding induction begins at n>1. The displayed estimates are printed with sup norms, not pointwise lower bounds. Retained.
147: The polynomial criterion for a smooth projective hypersurface is written “if (and only if)” without a reduced-polynomial hypothesis. Retained.
149: The upper-right block B is called a k by n matrix although its position requires k by (n-k). Retained.
150: The flag-manifold dimension sum ends at k-1 in the original. Retained.
152: The affine formula is printed F(z)=Ax+b. Retained.
155: The lifted function is inconsistently denoted by m or tilde m, and the proof prints tilde m=pi m although earlier the composition is m pi. Retained.
157: The original defines G_j=sum' omega^(-2j), then uses G_(2j+2) and later g_2=60G_4, g_3=140G_6; these conventions are inconsistent. The displayed Laurent expansion of wp also visibly lacks the plus sign between z^(-2) and the sum. Both are transcribed as visible in the scan.
158: The proof prints wp(omega_1+omega_2/2), without parentheses around omega_1+omega_2. Retained.
162: The source calls the endomorphisms “continuous homomorphisms” in the complex-multiplication exercise. The wp symbols in the addition formula are poorly reproduced in the scan but have been restored from the formula's immediately preceding context.
163: The chart in the quotient-manifold proof is printed with phi^(-1)(pi|U)^(-1), rather than phi(pi|U)^(-1). Retained.
164: The first printed definition of g_t has (az_0+z,az_1); a few lines later the family is written (az_0+tz_1,az_1). The total space is printed (C^2 times {0}) times D/Gamma rather than a punctured product. Both first expressions are retained as printed.
166: The quotient in the exotic-structure example is printed with a backslash before Gamma. Retained.
167: The Iwasawa exercise states a linear matrix group acts freely on C^3, without specifying the usual affine/group action. Retained.
168: The proof of Theorem 4.6.4 prints I_y(V)=(g_y); the surrounding argument concerns I_y(X). Retained.
169: The pulled-back hypersurfaces phi^(-1)(Z_j) are said to be “in D”; the target should naturally be U. Retained.
171–172: Theorem 4.6.8 and Definition 4.6.8 share the same number in the source. Both labels are retained.
179: The last clause of Lemma 4.7.1 states the extension map in the direction U to Sigma although the ensuing construction uses the reverse direction. Retained.
182: In the displayed zero locus defining tilde Z_2, the source prints two minus signs; the following equation uses the mathematically expected difference on its right-hand side. Retained.
183: The normal-crossing discussion prints I_x(Y)=(p_1,...,p_k), i.e. an ideal generated by the local component equations, instead of their product. Retained.
184: The embedded-resolution paragraph writes tilde X=pi^(-1)(X), whereas the intended smooth object is the strict transform. Retained.

The source includes historical assertions and terminology. They have not been updated to modern results.
